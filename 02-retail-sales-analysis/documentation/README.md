# Project 02 – Business Analysis Documentation

## UK Online Retail Sales Performance Analysis

**Status:** Completed – November 2026  
**Role:** Business Analyst | Data Analyst  
**Tools:** SQL (SQLite), Power BI, DB Browser for SQLite  
**Data Source:** UCI Online Retail Dataset (December 2010 – December 2011)

## Project Overview

This independent Business Analyst case study investigates historical retail transaction data to evaluate sales performance, product demand, customer purchasing behaviour and geographic market performance.

The project combines SQL analysis, data validation, business interpretation and Power BI reporting to develop evidence-based recommendations.

## Business Analysis Documentation

The full case study includes:

- Business problem and objectives
- Business questions and KPI definitions
- Data quality assessment and cleaning methodology
- Sales performance and monthly trends
- Product revenue, sales volume and unusual transaction analysis
- Customer segmentation and purchasing frequency
- Geographic sales performance and market comparisons
- Business recommendations and analysis limitations

## Key Results

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Qualifying Sales Revenue | £10,666,684.54 |
| Total Orders | 19,960 |
| Average Order Value | £534.40 |
| Units Sold | 5,588,376 |
| Identifiable Customers | 4,338 |

The reported revenue represents qualifying positive sales, not net revenue or profit.

## Project Limitations

- The analysis uses historical publicly available data.
- Cancelled transactions and non-positive sales are excluded from qualifying sales KPIs.
- Missing CustomerIDs limit customer-level analysis.
- Exceptional bulk transactions can influence product and customer rankings.
- Recommendations are analytical proposals and have not been implemented by the original retailer.
