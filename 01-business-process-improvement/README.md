# Customer Service Process Improvement

## Project Status
Planned

## Business Scenario
A fictional UK retail company is experiencing
delays in customer orders and customer complaints.

## Business Objectives
- Understand the current process.
- Identify operational bottlenecks.
- Develop improvement recommendations.
- Define measurable KPIs.

## Deliverables
- Project brief
- Stakeholder analysis
- AS-IS process map
- TO-BE process map
- Business requirements
- KPI framework
- Final recommendations
